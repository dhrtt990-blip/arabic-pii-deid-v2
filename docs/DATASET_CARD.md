# Dataset card: Arabic PII de-identification

This card describes two distinct components: the **planned training corpus** for fine-tuning and the **approved, frozen Golden Test** for evaluation. All future training/dev data will be screened against the frozen test before training.

## Golden test

- File: `data/golden_test.jsonl`, **100 unique, approved and frozen rows**.
- Written by the team following `docs/annotation_guidelines.md`: synthetic clinical and administrative sentences in Modern Standard Arabic and Saudi dialect. Every person, number and address is invented.
- Each of the four members authored and annotated 25 rows and reviewed 25 rows written by another member; authors and checkers differ on all rows. One entity annotation was corrected in `g090` before approval.
- Frozen before planned model training on **17 November 2026**: [commit 469984c00d101512df09851ebd61ea3ef096690e](https://github.com/dhrtt990-blip/arabic-pii-deid-v2/commit/469984c00d101512df09851ebd61ea3ef096690e), SHA-256 `9297f0d21c44ad7f7711af3c254a1e5ff9da1cf863e93162386151993d590459`.
- Row format: `{"id", "text", "entities": [{"text", "type"}], "dialect", "category", "written_by", "checked_by"}`.
- Entity counts: `PERS` 19, `LOC` 15, `ORG` 18, `DATE` 14, `NATIONAL_ID` 13, `PHONE` 15, `MRN` 13, `EMAIL` 14 (**121 spans**); **20** rows contain no entity and **35** are Saudi dialect.

## Training set

To be built by `src/data_prep.py` before fine-tuning. Every resulting row will be a chat example: the system prompt, the sentence, and the answer as JSON with the `PERS`, `LOC`, `ORG` and `DATE` entities. Identifiers (ID, phone, MRN, e-mail) are not training targets; the regex tools handle them.

### Processing

1. Token/tag data is converted to running text with character offsets; nested and overlapping entities are reduced to the outermost span.
2. Wojood's DATE label also covers vague expressions ("اليوم", a year on its own). Those are dropped so that the training target matches the annotation guidelines; the report gives the number dropped.
3. Sentences shorter than 3 words or longer than 600 characters are removed, then exact duplicates after normalisation.
4. Sentences that collide with the golden test are removed (next section).
5. Sentences without entities are capped at 25% of the set.
6. The set is shuffled with a fixed seed and split 90% / 10% into train and validation. The validation split comes from the same sources as training and is used only to watch the loss; it is not a test set.

## How the golden test is kept out of training

`src/data_prep.py` refuses to run unless the golden test exists and passes validation. It then compares every candidate training sentence with every golden sentence after normalisation (diacritics, tatweel and zero-width characters removed; Arabic-Indic digits mapped to ASCII; alef, yaa and taa marbuta variants unified; punctuation removed) and removes a candidate if:

- it equals a golden sentence;
- one contains the other (10 characters or more);
- the Jaccard similarity of their character 5-grams is 0.8 or higher;
- the shorter of the two shares 60% or more of its character 5-grams with the other, which catches a golden sentence with a word changed and a clause added.

Known limit of the check: a training sentence that keeps only the frame of a golden sentence and replaces its names and dates can share less than 60% and pass. This is why the golden test must not be written from the generator's templates.

After filtering, the final set is checked again and the script stops with an error if a single collision remains. The worked examples in the baseline prompt (`src/prompts.py`) are checked against the golden test in the same way.

## Personal data

The golden test, the template sentences and the team-written sentences contain no real personal data. ANERCorp and Wojood are published research corpora built from public text such as news, and name public figures as that text does.

## Limitations

- The training text is mostly news while the test is clinical and administrative.
- Template sentences follow a limited set of patterns; a model can learn the patterns rather than the task. The golden test must not be written from the same templates.
- ANERCorp has no dates. Date coverage comes from the Wojood sample and the templates only.
- Dialect coverage in training is thin compared with the golden test.
